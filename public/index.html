You may be here because…

Something in your life feels harder than it needs to.

You do not need to have everything figured out before starting therapy. We can begin with what is happening now and work from there.

01

Your mind rarely switches off.

Worry, overthinking, tension, or fear keep taking up more space than you want.

02

You feel depleted or disconnected.

Low mood, stress, burnout, or major changes have made it harder to feel like yourself.

03

Past experiences still show up today.

Trauma, painful memories, or old patterns continue to affect your emotions and relationships.

04

Your relationships feel stuck.

Communication, boundaries, conflict, family expectations, or cross-cultural differences are weighing on you.

05

You are between versions of yourself.

Life transitions, identity questions, migration, school, work, pregnancy, or postpartum changes have shifted your footing.

06

You want to understand yourself more deeply.

Therapy can also be a place for reflection, self-exploration, growth, and intentional change.

Meet Tess

You deserve therapy that feels human, not formulaic.

I’m Tess Zhao, a Licensed Clinical Social Worker providing psychotherapy in English and Mandarin.

My work is grounded in empathy, cultural sensitivity, and trauma-informed care. I prioritize a therapeutic relationship where you can feel safe enough to be honest, curious, and fully yourself.

We will make room for both insight and practical change—understanding the patterns that brought you here while building tools that can support you outside the therapy room.

What sessions can feel like

Insight and practical support, at a pace that fits you.

1

Understand what is happening. We identify the concerns, patterns, contexts, and goals that matter most.

2

Build a sense of safety and trust. Early work focuses on understanding your perspective and developing useful coping strategies.

3

Move into deeper work when you are ready. Therapy may include reflection, processing past hurts, strengthening relationships, and clarifying the future you want to build.
